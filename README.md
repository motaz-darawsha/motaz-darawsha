### Who I am

Backend engineer. I spend most of my time in **Node.js** and **TypeScript** — Discord bots, music/queue systems, and APIs that have to survive concurrent users, rate limits, and bad days.

I like small modules, explicit errors, and logs that actually help at 3am.
